Gift Reminder Privacy Policy

Operator and privacy contact: appdaman / appdaman1234@gmail.com
Effective date: November 5, 2026
English translation prepared November 18, 2026

1. Gift card information and storage
Gift card image copies, names, expiration dates, barcodes, notes, balances, usage records and reminder settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device to manage gift cards and reminders. Text and barcode recognition also run on your device. No account is required. Gift card content is not uploaded to the operator's server or included in advertising requests.

Deleting a gift card removes its information and image copies that are no longer used. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes internal app data. The app does not delete or modify gallery originals. Exported image files are separate copies in the location you choose. Automatic backup and cross-device synchronization are not provided. The operator cannot remotely access or recover gift card information stored only on your device.

2. Photos and notification permissions
The app reads only images you select through a photo/file picker or share with the app. It does not request access to your entire photo library, camera, contacts, messages or device location. Notification and exact-alarm permissions are used for expiration reminders. You can manage gift cards without granting these permissions.

3. Google services and advertising
The app uses Google AdMob for banner advertising and Google ML Kit for text and barcode recognition.
• AdMob may collect or share IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), app and advertising interactions, performance and diagnostic information, advertising IDs, app set IDs and other identifiers to provide and measure ads, perform analytics, and prevent fraud.
• ML Kit may send technical information to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics, including installation identifiers, device/app information, feature usage, performance/errors, and input format/size. Image content is processed on your device.

Google SDK data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption. Data may be processed on servers outside your country and retained or deleted under Google's policies.
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
AdMob data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure
ML Kit data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

Google privacy messages may appear depending on your region and consent status. When privacy choices are available, you can change them through Ad privacy choices in Settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in device settings. Declining personalized advertising does not stop all SDK communication.

4. External maps and contact
Selecting a store search sends the brand or store search term to an external map app or website. Location processing follows that service's permissions and policies; Gift Reminder does not collect your location.
If you email the operator, your email address and the information you provide are used to respond and resolve the issue, then deleted without undue delay unless retention is required by law. You can contact the email address above about access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests.

5. Protection and changes
Gift card information is protected by private app storage and Android access controls. Policy changes will be reflected in the app and public policy page together with their effective date.
